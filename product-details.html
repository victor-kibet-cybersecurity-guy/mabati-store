Care and Installation

  • Store roofing sheets in a dry, covered location before installation.
  • Use suitable roofing screws, washers, nails, and accessories.
  • Avoid dragging coated sheets across rough surfaces.
  • Confirm overlaps, ridges, valleys, flashing, and roof pitch.
  • Use a qualified roofing installer for final installation.
