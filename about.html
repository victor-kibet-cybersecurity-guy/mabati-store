Who We Are

Roofing Solutions for Homes, Businesses, and Institutions

ROYAL MABATI FACTORY LTD supplies mabati, roofing sheets, and roofing accessories for residential, commercial, institutional, agricultural, and industrial projects across Kenya.

We support customers in selecting roofing profiles, gauges, colours, finishes, sheet lengths, and accessories suited to their project requirements.

Our goal is to make roofing material selection, quotation, ordering, and delivery simple and clear.
