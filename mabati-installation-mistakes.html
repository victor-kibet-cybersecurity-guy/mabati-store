Common Mabati Installation Mistakes

Good roofing sheets still perform poorly when installed incorrectly. Careful measurement, support preparation and water management matter throughout installation.

By ROYAL MABATI FACTORY LTD8 August 2026Kenya roofing guide

Good roofing sheets still perform poorly when installed incorrectly. Careful measurement, support preparation and water management matter throughout installation.

Incorrect measurements

Ordering before verifying roof dimensions creates shortages, unnecessary cutting and waste.

Wrong overlap

Insufficient side or end overlap increases leakage risk. Use the requirements for the selected roofing profile.

Poor fastener placement

Incorrect screws, nails, washers or placement can damage sheets and create water entry points.

Weak or uneven supports

Roofing sheets need a straight, properly spaced support structure. Uneven purlins can distort the finished roof.

Ignoring flashing and drainage

Ridges, valleys, gutters, barge flashing and penetrations need careful detailing to direct rainwater away from the building.
