Gauge 28 vs Gauge 30 Mabati in Kenya

Gauge is one of the most important specifications to check when comparing mabati. Gauge 28 and Gauge 30 often serve different budgets and project requirements.

By ROYAL MABATI FACTORY LTD8 August 2026Kenya roofing guide

Gauge is one of the most important specifications to check when comparing mabati. Gauge 28 and Gauge 30 often serve different budgets and project requirements.

What gauge means

Gauge is used to describe sheet thickness. When comparing products, confirm the manufacturer's actual thickness specification because naming and tolerances matter.

Gauge 28

Gauge 28 is generally selected when buyers want a thicker roofing sheet and are willing to spend more for the added material.

Gauge 30

Gauge 30 is generally a lower-cost option and is widely used where the project design and support system suit the specification.

Do not compare gauge alone

Also compare coating, steel quality, profile, finish, warranty terms, support spacing and installation quality.

Best buying approach

Request a written quotation showing profile, gauge, finish, length, price per metre, accessories and delivery details.
