Mabati Roof Maintenance Guide Kenya

Routine roof checks help you spot small issues before they become leaks or structural repairs. Inspect the roof after severe weather and at regular intervals.

By ROYAL MABATI FACTORY LTD8 August 2026Kenya roofing guide

Routine roof checks help you spot small issues before they become leaks or structural repairs. Inspect the roof after severe weather and at regular intervals.

Keep gutters and valleys clear

Remove leaves, soil and debris so rainwater drains freely.

Inspect fasteners

Check for loose, missing or damaged fasteners and worn sealing washers.

Look for coating damage

Scratches, exposed metal and corrosion should receive suitable treatment before the affected area grows.

Check flashing and ridge caps

Inspect joints around ridges, valleys, walls, chimneys and other penetrations.

Use safe access

Avoid walking on roofing without proper safety equipment and knowledge of safe load points. Use a qualified roofer for difficult repairs.
