Roofing Quotation Checklist Kenya

A clear quotation makes roofing offers easier to compare. Ask for enough detail to understand what is included before placing an order.

By ROYAL MABATI FACTORY LTD8 August 2026Kenya roofing guide

A clear quotation makes roofing offers easier to compare. Ask for enough detail to understand what is included before placing an order.

Product specification

Confirm roofing profile, gauge, coating, finish, colour and brand or supplier description.

Lengths and quantities

The quotation should show sheet lengths, quantities and price basis, such as price per metre.

Accessories

List ridges, valleys, gutters, downpipes, flashing, screws, nails and other required items separately.

Delivery

Confirm delivery destination, delivery charge or promotion, expected process and offloading arrangements.

Payment and confirmation

Verify the seller's current payment instructions directly before sending money. Keep the quotation and transaction records.

Useful next steps

Compare the relevant roofing products and accessories, estimate your quantities, then request a written quotation before ordering.
