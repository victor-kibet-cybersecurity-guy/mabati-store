How to Choose Mabati in Kenya

Choosing roofing sheets is easier when you compare the parts of the product that affect appearance, strength, installation and total project cost.

By ROYAL MABATI FACTORY LTD8 August 2026Kenya roofing guide

Choosing roofing sheets is easier when you compare the parts of the product that affect appearance, strength, installation and total project cost.

Start with the roof design

Match the roofing profile to the building style, roof pitch and drainage needs. Box profile works well on many homes and commercial buildings. Corrugated sheets remain a practical option for homes, farm structures and utility buildings.

Compare Gauge 28 and Gauge 30

Gauge affects sheet thickness and price. Do not choose from price alone. Consider span, support spacing, building type and expected service conditions. Confirm the exact specification before ordering.

Choose the finish and colour

Matte and glossy finishes create different appearances. Popular Kenyan roof colours include charcoal grey, brick red, forest green, coffee brown and natural silver. Match the colour to walls, climate and long-term maintenance preferences.

Budget for the complete roof

Include ridges, valleys, gutters, flashing, nails, screws and transport in your estimate. A low sheet price does not always produce the lowest completed-roof cost.

Confirm measurements before ordering

Use roof dimensions to estimate quantities, then verify the measurements before cutting custom lengths. This reduces waste and shortage risk.

Useful next steps

Compare the relevant roofing products and accessories, estimate your quantities, then request a written quotation before ordering.
