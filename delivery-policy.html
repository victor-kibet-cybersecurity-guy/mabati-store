Delivery Policy

This policy explains how roofing orders are prepared, dispatched, delivered, received, inspected, and reported.

1. Overview

This Delivery Policy applies to roofing sheets, mabati, ridge caps, valleys, gutters, flashing, fasteners, insulation, and other roofing accessories supplied by ROYAL MABATI FACTORY LTD.

Delivery arrangements form part of the confirmed quotation or order. Customers should read this policy together with our Terms and Conditions.

2. Delivery Coverage

ROYAL MABATI FACTORY LTD arranges delivery to customers across all 47 counties in Kenya, including towns, estates, rural locations, construction sites, schools, churches, farms, warehouses, and commercial projects.

Delivery availability may depend on:

  • Road and vehicle access
  • Security conditions
  • Weather conditions
  • Order size
  • Product availability
  • Transport availability
  • Distance from the dispatch point

Remote or difficult-to-access locations may require additional arrangements or an alternative delivery point.

3. Free Nationwide Delivery

Free delivery may be offered for qualifying confirmed orders across Kenya.

Eligibility may depend on:

  • Minimum order quantity or value
  • Product category
  • Delivery destination
  • Vehicle and route availability
  • Road accessibility
  • Whether the order is combined with other deliveries

Free delivery does not automatically include:

  • Manual offloading labour
  • Crane, forklift, or lifting equipment
  • Carrying products beyond the vehicle drop point
  • Multiple delivery attempts
  • Special access permits
  • Additional trips caused by incorrect customer information

The confirmed quotation will state the applicable delivery terms for the order.

4. Order Confirmation Before Delivery

Delivery is arranged only after the order has been confirmed. Confirmation includes reviewing:

  • Product names and roofing profiles
  • Gauge and colour
  • Sheet lengths
  • Quantities
  • Accessories
  • Current prices
  • Customer contact information
  • Delivery location
  • Payment status

A product saved in the website cart or wishlist is not reserved and does not create a confirmed delivery.

5. Order Preparation and Dispatch

Before dispatch, roofing materials may be:

  • Custom-cut to confirmed lengths
  • Counted and checked
  • Grouped by profile, gauge, colour, or size
  • Loaded with suitable handling precautions
  • Matched against the quotation or delivery note

Preparation begins after the order, measurements, payment requirements, and delivery details are confirmed.

Customers should not request final cutting until all roof measurements have been verified.

6. Delivery Timelines

Delivery dates and times are estimates unless expressly confirmed in writing.

Delivery timing may depend on:

  • Stock availability
  • Custom cutting or fabrication
  • Order size
  • Loading schedule
  • Destination
  • Traffic and road conditions
  • Weather
  • Transport coordination

Customers will be informed where a significant known delay affects the expected delivery.

7. Customer Delivery Information

Customers must provide complete and accurate delivery details:

  • Full name
  • Phone and WhatsApp number
  • County
  • Town or trading centre
  • Road, estate, village, or area
  • Nearby landmark
  • Exact delivery point
  • Site contact person
  • Preferred delivery date
  • Road-access information

ROYAL MABATI FACTORY LTD is not responsible for delays or additional costs caused by incorrect, incomplete, or misleading delivery information.

8. Site and Road Access

The customer must confirm that the delivery location is safely accessible by the assigned vehicle.

Important access information includes:

  • Narrow roads
  • Low bridges or power lines
  • Weight restrictions
  • Muddy or unpaved roads
  • Steep slopes
  • Construction-site hazards
  • Security gates or access permits
  • Restricted delivery hours

The driver may decline to enter an unsafe or inaccessible road. In such cases, delivery may be completed at the nearest safe and agreed location.

9. Offloading and Handling

Unless expressly included in the confirmed quotation, the customer is responsible for arranging sufficient labour to offload roofing materials safely.

The receiving area should be:

  • Clear and level
  • Dry where possible
  • Free from sharp objects
  • Large enough for the materials
  • Accessible without unsafe carrying distances

Roofing sheets should not be dragged across rough ground or each other, because this may damage coatings or surfaces.

Special lifting equipment, cranes, forklifts, or extra offloading labour may attract additional charges.

10. Inspection at Delivery

The customer or authorized receiver should inspect the order immediately upon delivery and compare it with the quotation, invoice, or delivery note.

The inspection should confirm:

  • Product profile
  • Gauge
  • Colour
  • Sheet length
  • Quantity
  • Accessories
  • Visible product condition

Signing a delivery note without recording a visible shortage or damage may be treated as confirmation that the order was received in apparent good condition, subject to rights that cannot legally be excluded.

11. Damage, Incorrect Items, or Shortages

Any visible damage, incorrect product, or shortage should be reported immediately before the delivery vehicle leaves, where reasonably possible.

The customer should provide:

  • Order or quotation reference
  • Description of the issue
  • Photographs of the affected materials
  • Photographs of packaging or loading condition
  • Quantity affected
  • Delivery location and date

Products should not be installed, cut, altered, or used before a reported issue is reviewed.

Claims will be assessed based on the confirmed order, delivery records, product condition, and applicable law.

12. Failed Delivery Attempts

A delivery attempt may fail where:

  • The customer or receiver is unavailable
  • The phone number cannot be reached
  • The location details are incorrect
  • The road is unsafe or inaccessible
  • The site refuses delivery
  • Offloading arrangements are unavailable
  • Required payment has not been confirmed

A new delivery date may need to be arranged. Additional transport, storage, or handling charges may apply where the failed delivery was caused by the customer.

13. Changes to the Delivery Location

Any request to change the delivery location must be made as early as possible.

A change is subject to:

  • Approval
  • Vehicle route
  • Distance
  • Road access
  • Dispatch status
  • Revised delivery cost or timing

A delivery location may not be changed after dispatch where the new route is unsafe, impracticable, or significantly different from the confirmed destination.

14. Delivery Delays

Delivery may be delayed by circumstances outside our reasonable control, including:

  • Severe weather
  • Flooding
  • Road closures
  • Vehicle breakdown
  • Traffic disruption
  • Security incidents
  • Government restrictions
  • Supplier or production delays
  • Network or communication failure

We will take reasonable steps to communicate known delays and arrange the earliest practical alternative.

15. Ownership and Risk

Ownership and risk in the goods will pass according to the confirmed quotation, payment terms, and applicable law.

After delivery and acceptance, the customer is responsible for safe storage, handling, protection, and installation.

Roofing materials should be kept:

  • Dry and covered
  • Raised above wet ground
  • Away from chemicals and cement dust
  • Protected from scratching and impact
  • Secure from theft or unauthorized use

16. Customer Collection

Where customer collection is agreed, the customer must provide suitable transport, secure loading support, and an authorized representative.

The customer should verify the order before leaving the collection point.

ROYAL MABATI FACTORY LTD is not responsible for damage caused by unsuitable customer transport, insecure loading, improper tying, overloading, or poor handling after collection.

17. Returns After Delivery

Return or exchange requests are assessed under our Terms and Conditions.

Custom-cut, made-to-order, installed, altered, used, or damaged products may not be returnable except where required by law or where the product is defective or does not match the confirmed order.

Customers should contact us before transporting any item back. Unauthorized returns may be refused.

18. Delivery and Site Safety

Customers must maintain a safe receiving environment. Children, unauthorized persons, animals, and unnecessary vehicles should be kept away from the unloading area.

The driver or delivery team may suspend unloading where there is a serious safety risk.

Roofing sheets can have sharp edges. Gloves and suitable protective equipment should be used when handling them.
