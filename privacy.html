1. Introduction

ROYAL MABATI FACTORY LTD respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, disclosed, and protected when you use our website, contact us, request a quotation, place an order, use WhatsApp, call our sales team, subscribe to updates, or interact with our online services.

This policy is intended to support compliance with applicable Kenyan data-protection requirements, including the Data Protection Act, 2019 and related regulations.

By using this website or voluntarily submitting personal information, you acknowledge that you have read this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect the following categories of personal information:

2.1 Contact information

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • WhatsApp number
  • Email address
  • County, town, road, estate, or delivery location

2.2 Order and quotation information

  • Selected roofing products
  • Product quantities
  • Sheet lengths
  • Roofing profiles
  • Gauge and colour preferences
  • Roofing accessories
  • Project type and project location
  • Preferred delivery date
  • Messages and special instructions

2.3 Transaction information

  • Quotation references
  • Order status
  • Payment confirmation details
  • Delivery and receipt information

We do not request that customers submit card PINs, mobile-money PINs, passwords, or other secret authentication credentials through the website.

2.4 Website and device information

When analytics or similar tools are enabled, limited technical information may be collected, such as:

  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited
  • Referral source
  • Approximate location based on network information
  • Date and time of access

3. How We Collect Information

We may collect personal information when you:

  • Complete a quotation or delivery form
  • Add products to your cart or wishlist
  • Send an order through WhatsApp
  • Call or message our sales team
  • Subscribe to roofing updates
  • Request customer support
  • Provide delivery information
  • Interact with website analytics or security tools

Some information is collected directly from you, while limited technical data may be collected automatically by your browser, website-hosting provider, or approved analytics services.

4. How We Use Personal Information

We may use personal information for the following purposes:

  • Responding to product enquiries
  • Preparing quotations
  • Processing and confirming orders
  • Calculating estimated order totals
  • Arranging delivery
  • Communicating about product availability
  • Providing customer support
  • Maintaining cart and wishlist functionality
  • Preventing fraud or misuse
  • Improving website usability and performance
  • Meeting legal, accounting, and regulatory obligations
  • Sending requested roofing updates or promotional messages

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

5. Lawful Basis for Processing

We process personal information only where there is an appropriate reason to do so. Depending on the circumstances, this may include:

  • Your consent
  • Steps taken at your request before entering into a contract
  • Performance of an order or other agreement
  • Compliance with a legal obligation
  • Legitimate business interests that do not override your rights

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent, subject to applicable legal or contractual requirements.

6. When We May Share Information

We may share limited personal information only where necessary for legitimate business or legal purposes, including with:

  • Delivery and transport providers
  • Payment-service providers
  • Website-hosting providers
  • IT and security-service providers
  • Professional advisers
  • Government authorities where required by law

Service providers should receive only the information necessary to perform their assigned function and should handle it securely.

7. Storage and Retention

Personal information may be stored electronically or in business records used to process quotations, orders, customer support, accounting, and delivery.

We aim to keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including:

  • Completing an enquiry or transaction
  • Maintaining order and delivery records
  • Meeting tax, accounting, or legal obligations
  • Resolving disputes
  • Preventing fraud or misuse

Information that is no longer required should be securely deleted, anonymized, or archived where retention is legally necessary.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect personal information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

These measures may include access controls, secure hosting, software updates, restricted staff access, and careful handling of customer records.

No internet transmission or electronic storage method is completely secure. Customers should avoid sending passwords, PINs, or other highly sensitive credentials through website forms, WhatsApp, email, or social media.

9. Cookies and Browser Storage

This frontend website may use browser storage to support features such as:

  • Shopping cart items
  • Wishlist items
  • Recently viewed products
  • Roofing calculator entries
  • Newsletter preferences

This information may remain on your device until you clear your browser data or the website removes it.

If analytics, advertising, or third-party cookies are added later, the site should provide appropriate notice and consent controls where required.

10. Third-Party Services and Links

The website may link to or use third-party services such as:

  • WhatsApp
  • Telephone services
  • Google Fonts
  • Font Awesome
  • Website-hosting providers
  • Map or analytics services

These third parties operate under their own privacy policies. ROYAL MABATI FACTORY LTD is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites or platforms.

Customers should review the privacy terms of any external service before submitting personal information.

11. Your Data-Protection Rights

Subject to applicable Kenyan law, you may have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your personal data is used
  • Request access to personal data held about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion of information where legally appropriate
  • Object to certain forms of processing
  • Request restriction of processing
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Request information about third parties receiving your data

To make a request, contact us using the phone or WhatsApp details provided below. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

12. Children's Privacy

Our website and roofing services are intended for adults and businesses. We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children for commercial transactions.

If you believe a child has submitted personal information without appropriate authorization, contact us so that we can review and remove it where appropriate.

13. International Data Transfers

Some third-party technology providers may process or store information outside Kenya. Where cross-border processing occurs, reasonable steps should be taken to ensure appropriate safeguards and compliance with applicable data-protection requirements.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our services, website features, technology, legal requirements, or business practices.

The latest version will be posted on this page with the updated effective date.
