Roofing Accessories Checklist Kenya

A complete roofing quotation includes more than sheets. Missing accessories delay installation and make the final roof harder to weatherproof.

By ROYAL MABATI FACTORY LTD8 August 2026Kenya roofing guide

A complete roofing quotation includes more than sheets. Missing accessories delay installation and make the final roof harder to weatherproof.

Ridge caps

Ridges cover the meeting point at the top of roof slopes and help keep water out.

Valleys and flashing

Valleys carry water where roof sections meet. Flashing protects edges, wall junctions and penetrations.

Fasteners

Use suitable roofing screws or nails with the correct washers for the sheet and support material.

Gutters and downpipes

Gutters collect roof runoff. Downpipes move water away from walls and foundations.

Finishing accessories

Depending on the roof, you may also need barge flashing, closures, brackets, sealants and other profile-specific components.
