Box Profile vs Corrugated Mabati in Kenya

Box profile and corrugated mabati are both common roofing choices in Kenya. The better option depends on your building, preferred appearance, budget and specification.

By ROYAL MABATI FACTORY LTD8 August 2026Kenya roofing guide

Box profile and corrugated mabati are both common roofing choices in Kenya. The better option depends on your building, preferred appearance, budget and specification.

Box profile mabati

Box profile sheets use wider, angular ribs. They suit many modern homes, shops, warehouses and commercial projects.

Corrugated mabati

Corrugated sheets use repeating rounded waves. They remain common for residential, agricultural and utility structures.

Appearance

Box profile usually creates a sharper modern roof line. Corrugated roofing produces the familiar traditional iron-sheet appearance.

Installation and support

Both profiles require correct support spacing, overlaps, fasteners and flashing. Follow the selected sheet specification rather than assuming one installation method fits every profile.

Which should you choose?

Choose according to roof design, gauge, coating, available colours, budget and the finish you want. Compare complete quotations instead of sheet price alone.
