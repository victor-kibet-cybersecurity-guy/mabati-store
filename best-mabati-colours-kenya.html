Best Mabati Colours in Kenya

Roof colour affects the look of the whole building. Choose a colour that works with the walls, surroundings, roof profile and maintenance expectations.

By ROYAL MABATI FACTORY LTD8 August 2026Kenya roofing guide

Roof colour affects the look of the whole building. Choose a colour that works with the walls, surroundings, roof profile and maintenance expectations.

Charcoal grey

A common modern choice that pairs with white, cream, grey, stone and many bright accent colours.

Brick red

Works well with cream, beige, light grey and earthy exterior finishes.

Forest green

Often suits rural, landscaped and natural settings and pairs with neutral wall colours.

Coffee brown

Creates a warm neutral roof and works with cream, tan and stone-inspired walls.

Natural silver

A practical metallic appearance suited to many utility, agricultural and industrial buildings.
