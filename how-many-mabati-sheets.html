How Many Mabati Sheets Do I Need?

A useful roofing estimate starts with accurate roof measurements. Sheet quantity depends on the roof shape and the effective cover width of the selected profile.

By ROYAL MABATI FACTORY LTD8 August 2026Kenya roofing guide

A useful roofing estimate starts with accurate roof measurements. Sheet quantity depends on the roof shape and the effective cover width of the selected profile.

Measure each roof section

Record the slope length from ridge to eave and the horizontal length of each roof section. Complex roofs should be broken into simple sections.

Use effective cover width

Do not divide by total sheet width. Use the effective cover width after side overlap. This value varies by profile.

Account for hips, valleys and overlaps

Hip roofs and roofs with valleys need more cutting than simple gable roofs. Allow for practical waste.

Estimate sheet count

For a simple roof section, divide the horizontal roof length by the effective cover width and round up. Repeat for every roof section.

Confirm before production

Custom-length sheets should be cut only after the final site dimensions have been checked.
