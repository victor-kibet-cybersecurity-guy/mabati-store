Quick Roofing Tips

Before You Order Mabati

These simple checks can help reduce ordering mistakes and unexpected project costs.

Confirm Roof Measurements

Use accurate dimensions and account for roof pitch, overlaps, ridges, valleys, and wastage.

Confirm the Gauge

Check the product specification and ensure the selected gauge matches your needs.

Confirm Colour Availability

Colour availability may vary by profile, gauge, coating, finish, and stock status.

Include Accessories

Add ridges, valleys, flashing, fasteners, gutters, downpipes, sealants, and insulation.

Review the Quotation

Confirm products, quantities, prices, delivery details, and payment instructions.

Use a Qualified Installer

Proper installation helps protect roofing materials and improve long-term performance.
