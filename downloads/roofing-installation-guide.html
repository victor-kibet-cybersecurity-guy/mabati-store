Roofing Installation Guide

This general guide does not replace a qualified roofing installer, structural engineer, or the product manufacturer's written instructions.

Before installation

  • Confirm roof measurements, pitch, purlin spacing, sheet lengths, overlaps, ridges and valleys.
  • Store sheets dry, raised above the ground, covered and ventilated.
  • Confirm the correct fasteners, washers, flashing and sealants.

Handling

  • Lift sheets instead of dragging them.
  • Use gloves because sheet edges may be sharp.
  • Remove metal filings after drilling or cutting.

Installation

  • Begin from the correct roof edge based on prevailing weather conditions.
  • Maintain straight alignment and consistent side/end laps.
  • Use approved fasteners in the recommended positions.
  • Do not overtighten washers.
  • Install ridges, valleys and flashing to prevent water entry.
Confirm product-specific installation and warranty requirements before work begins.
